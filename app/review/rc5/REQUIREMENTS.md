# Requirements for VisitSheet rc5

The product description, from idea.md:

> VisitSheet helps a patient, or the person helping them, turn scattered notes into a one-page appointment brief: the three questions that matter most at the top, notes since last time in their own words, the medicine list exactly as they typed it, and how the clinic should communicate with them.

Sources, in order of authority for this build:

- `/workspace/pipeline/healthhack/idea.md` — product source. It overrides `/workspace/pipeline/healthhack/prd.md` and `/workspace/pipeline/healthhack/qa.md` where they conflict. On conflict, prd.md and qa.md lose.
- `/workspace/pipeline/healthhack/prd.md` and `/workspace/pipeline/healthhack/qa.md` — older spec. Do not follow them when they disagree with idea.md.
- `ACCEPTANCE.md` in this repo — the rc4 contract. It was not edited, not weakened, and not renumbered.
